Coverage & how to read this page

Southwest includes the Rainbow, Jones, Durango, Fort Apache and Blue Diamond corridors, with Flamingo as the northern edge. South includes Enterprise, Silverado Ranch, Town Square and the south Eastern corridor. Southwest Henderson focuses on Eastern / Anthem and nearby Green Valley South. Chinatown, the central Strip, Summerlin and eastern Henderson are outside the main search area.

This is a broad researched directory, not a certified census of every restaurant. Menu snapshots are short selections or categories, not full transcriptions. Open each linked menu for the complete offering. Prices are those displayed by the linked source when researched, may be undated, and exclude tax/tip; ordering-platform prices may differ from dine-in. AYCE means all-you-can-eat; prices shown for individual items are not AYCE admission prices.

Research notes

Restaurant websites and their ordering menus were preferred. Clearly labeled delivery menus and local menu listings fill gaps. Links are provided beside every restaurant so the information can be traced. No customer photographs or full menu images have been copied or republished. A Yelp link is included where used; this is not a claim that all Yelp menu photographs were examined.

Conflicting and historical listings: Older Bonsai and Sushi Hiro entries at addresses now listed by Ohjah Rainbow and Island Sushi were not counted as separate current businesses. Jjanga’s current address and discontinued hibachi service were checked against its own contact page. Kyara is listed under its current name.

Needs further confirmation: Ramen Kobo at 7040 S Durango Dr has an accessible official menu, but a recent local report says it closed. It is excluded from the active directory pending confirmation. Older Monta Chaya and Tonkatsu Kiyoshi listings were also excluded because current operation was not confidently established. Soru’s Henderson pages contain conflicting contact information, so that branch is excluded pending address confirmation.

Business operation has not been confirmed by telephone. Menus, locations and dining formats can change. This independent directory has no restaurant affiliation or ranking. Research is dated; the page does not update automatically.
