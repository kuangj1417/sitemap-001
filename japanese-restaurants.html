Coverage & how to use this page

Expanded edition: the original Southwest, South and SW Henderson coverage now includes Chinatown along Spring Mountain and Jones, nearby west-of-Strip dining, and west-side corridors including Decatur, Sahara, Desert Inn, Durango and Fort Apache. Area labels are practical browsing groups, not municipal boundaries. Separate branches count as separate locations.

Search by restaurant, dish or street, or filter by area and food style. Each snapshot is a short menu selection, not a full transcription; open its source for the complete menu. Prices are those displayed by the linked source when researched, may be undated, and can differ from dine-in prices. Tax and tip are extra. No price is invented where it could not be confirmed. This dated directory does not update automatically and is not guaranteed exhaustive.

Selection & research notes

Expanded coverage: Chinatown and west-side additions were researched on October 19, 2026. Restaurant websites and branch ordering menus are preferred; secondary menu listings and local reporting are labeled. Cards provide short menu summaries with links, not complete menu transcriptions. Prices and available items may vary by branch and service.

Opening status has not been confirmed by telephone. An online listing does not guarantee current operation. Confirm hours, reservations, special diets and package inclusions directly. This directory is not exhaustive and does not update automatically.

Fusion, mixed Chinese/Japanese menus and seafood restaurants with Japanese influences are labeled. Omakase and sushi menus can change with the day’s fish. AYCE, revolving sushi and à la carte are different formats; confirm the specific restaurant’s offering.

Temporarily closed: Yui Edomae Sushi currently states on its own website that it is temporarily closed without a confirmed reopening date. It is not included in the dining-location count.

No customer photographs or full menu images have been copied or republished. External links belong to their respective providers. Independent directory; no ranking or restaurant affiliation.
