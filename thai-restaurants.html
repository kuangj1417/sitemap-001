Coverage & how to use this page

Coverage includes southwest and south Las Vegas, southwest Henderson, Chinatown and west-side corridors. Area labels are practical browsing groups; they are not municipal boundaries. Separate branches count separately. Selected Maryland/UNLV and Tropicana restaurants are labeled East of Strip as a nearby extension. North Las Vegas and the broader east valley are outside this edition.

Search by restaurant, dish or street, or filter by area and food style. Each snapshot is a short menu selection, not a full transcription; open its source for the complete menu. Prices are those displayed by the linked source when researched, may be undated, and can differ from dine-in prices. Tax and tip are extra. No price is invented where it could not be confirmed. This dated directory does not update automatically and is not guaranteed exhaustive.
