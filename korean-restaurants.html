Coverage & how to use this page

Southwest covers Rainbow, Jones, Durango, Fort Apache and Blue Diamond, with a limited northern boundary at Flamingo. South covers Enterprise, Southern Highlands, Silverado Ranch, Town Square and the southern Eastern corridor. Southwest Henderson covers St Rose, Eastern, Anthem and Inspirada.

Search by restaurant, dish or street, or filter by area and food style. Each snapshot is a short menu selection, not a full transcription; open its source for the complete menu. Prices are those displayed by the linked source when researched, may be undated, and can differ from dine-in prices. Tax and tip are extra. No price is invented where it could not be confirmed. This dated directory does not update automatically and is not guaranteed exhaustive.

Selection & research notes

Sources favor restaurant sites and direct ordering pages, supplemented by labeled delivery menus and local listings. Each card links its sources. Some sources are undated; opening status has not been confirmed by telephone.

Names and uncertainty: Korea House is counted once rather than duplicating Made in Korea. Meat Salon is not duplicated as Honey Pig. Dynamite at 9711 S Eastern, H7 is excluded because newer Noodle Master listings use the same suite. Pelicana’s older direct-order URL returned no site; its card uses a delivery source and asks you to confirm operation.

Fusion and Korean-Chinese restaurants are labeled so you can filter them separately. BBQ does not automatically mean AYCE. For AYCE, confirm time limits, party-wide tier rules, child pricing and food-waste charges with the restaurant.

No customer photographs or full menu images have been copied or republished. External links belong to their respective providers. Independent directory; no ranking or restaurant affiliation.
