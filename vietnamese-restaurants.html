Coverage & how to use this page

Coverage includes southwest and south Las Vegas, southwest Henderson, Chinatown and west-side corridors. Area labels are practical browsing groups; they are not municipal boundaries. Separate branches count separately. Selected Maryland/UNLV and Tropicana restaurants are labeled East of Strip as a nearby extension. North Las Vegas and the broader east valley are outside this edition.

Search by restaurant, dish or street, or filter by area and food style. Each snapshot is a short menu selection, not a full transcription; open its source for the complete menu. Prices are those displayed by the linked source when researched, may be undated, and can differ from dine-in prices. Tax and tip are extra. No price is invented where it could not be confirmed. This dated directory does not update automatically and is not guaranteed exhaustive.

Selection & research notes

Researched October 19, 2026 using restaurant websites, local ordering menus and labeled secondary listings. Menu snapshots are short selections, not full transcriptions. No customer photos or full menu images are republished. Current operation has not been confirmed by telephone; check hours, branch and menu availability directly. This dated directory is not exhaustive and does not update automatically.

District One is omitted because local reporting documents its closure, despite its surviving website. Pho Aimie on South Rainbow is not confused with Pho A’mie in Fullerton, California. Pho Kim Long’s Spring Mountain address varies between 4023 and 4029 across sources; the restaurant ordering-page address is used and the location is counted once. Mang Felix is marked as a mixed Filipino/Vietnamese kitchen.

No customer photographs or full menu images have been copied or republished. External links belong to their respective providers. Independent directory; no ranking or restaurant affiliation.
