Coverage & how to use this page

Southwest covers Rainbow, Jones, Durango, Fort Apache and Blue Diamond, with a limited northern boundary at Flamingo. South covers Enterprise, Southern Highlands, Silverado Ranch, Town Square and the southern Eastern corridor. Southwest Henderson covers St Rose, Eastern, Anthem and Inspirada.

Search by restaurant, dish or street, or filter by area and food style. Each snapshot is a short menu selection, not a full transcription; open its source for the complete menu. Prices are those displayed by the linked source when researched, may be undated, and can differ from dine-in prices. Tax and tip are extra. No price is invented where it could not be confirmed. This dated directory does not update automatically and is not guaranteed exhaustive.

Selection & research notes

Selection rule: The list focuses on recognizable regional Chinese cooking—Cantonese seafood/roast meats/clay pots, Shanghainese dumplings, Sichuan dishes and handmade Chinese noodles. Panda Express, China A Go Go / China Go Go and predominantly American Chinese combination-plate takeout restaurants are excluded. A kitchen with substantial regional dishes may still offer orange chicken; its regional section is the reason for inclusion.

Mr Chopstix is explicitly marked as a mixed Chinese/Thai menu. YuXiang belongs on the Korean page under Korean-Chinese cuisine, rather than being presented here as a regional Chinese specialist. Pan-Asian casino noodle counters are excluded from this focused edition. Chinatown and the central Strip are outside the requested core area; familiar destinations there have not been added to inflate the count.

Conflicting records: Shàng Miàn / Shàng Artisan Noodle on Maryland is one location. The old Noodle Man domain redirected to unrelated content; the card uses a secondary menu, and the disputed Fort Apache branch is excluded. Noodle Master and older Dynamite listings share a suite; only the current noodle listing appears here. 18 Dough remains flagged for reconfirmation.

This is a menu-based selection, not a certification of authenticity or a claim that every qualifying restaurant has been found. Cuisine tags are descriptive; they do not identify ownership or a chef’s nationality.
