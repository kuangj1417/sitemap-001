Coverage & how to use this page

Expanded edition: the original Southwest, South and SW Henderson coverage now includes Chinatown along Spring Mountain and Jones, nearby restaurants west of the Strip, and the Sahara corridor. Area labels are practical browsing groups, not city boundaries. Separate branches count as separate locations.

Search by restaurant, dish or street, or filter by area and food style. Each snapshot is a short menu selection, not a full transcription; open its source for the complete menu. Prices are those displayed by the linked source when researched, may be undated, and can differ from dine-in prices. Tax and tip are extra. No price is invented where it could not be confirmed. This dated directory does not update automatically and is not guaranteed exhaustive.

Selection & research notes

Reported closed: Rainbow Kitchen was removed from active listings following the directory owner’s report on October 19, 2026. This closure has not been independently confirmed.

Expanded research: This edition adds Cantonese seafood and dim sum kitchens, Chinese barbecue, Sichuan cooking and specialist noodles across Chinatown and west-side corridors. SK Seafood, Imperial Sushi & Seafood Buffet, New Asian BBQ, Hong Kong Garden and Ondori are included.

Selection: Panda Express, China A Go Go / China Go Go and restaurants focused on American Chinese combination-plate takeout are excluded. Restaurants with substantial regional cooking can also serve familiar American Chinese dishes. Mixed menus are labeled: Imperial is a Chinese/Japanese buffet; Ondori serves Chinese and Japanese food; Mr Chopstix also serves Thai dishes. Cuisine tags describe the menu, not ownership or a chef’s nationality.

Sources: Restaurant websites and direct ordering menus are preferred; secondary menus and local reporting are labeled and linked. No restaurant has been confirmed by telephone. Menu snapshots are summaries, not complete menus. Listings and online ordering do not independently guarantee a restaurant is currently operating.

Conflicting records: MIAN is not added because current listings conflict with closure reports. The disputed Noodle Man Fort Apache branch is excluded; its former website redirected to unrelated content. Shàng Miàn on Maryland and Shàng Artisan Noodle are counted once. Noodle Master is not duplicated as older Dynamite at the same suite. 18 Dough remains marked for reconfirmation. The Buffalo dumpling restaurant is not assumed to share an ordering site with Chinatown Xiao Long Dumplings.

This is a researched directory, not a ranking or an exhaustive claim. Sources can lag openings, closures, menu changes and prices. Confirm hours, seafood market prices and branch details directly.

No customer photographs or full menu images have been copied or republished. External links belong to their respective providers. Independent directory; no ranking or restaurant affiliation.
