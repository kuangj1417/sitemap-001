Coverage & how to use this page

Coverage includes southwest and south Las Vegas, southwest Henderson, Chinatown and west-side corridors. Area labels are practical browsing groups; they are not municipal boundaries. Separate branches count separately. The Mediterranean directory also includes the two Istanbul restaurants on the Strip and Fremont Street as specifically requested additions. North Las Vegas is outside this edition.

Search by restaurant, dish or street, or filter by area and food style. Each snapshot is a short menu selection, not a full transcription; open its source for the complete menu. Prices are those displayed by the linked source when researched, may be undated, and can differ from dine-in prices. Tax and tip are extra. No price is invented where it could not be confirmed. This dated directory does not update automatically and is not guaranteed exhaustive.

Selection & research notes

Researched October 19, 2026 using restaurant websites, location pages and linked menus. Secondary sources supplement listings where noted. Short menu snapshots are examples, not complete menus; check the branch ordering page for current dishes and prices. Opening status has not been confirmed by telephone. This directory is not exhaustive and does not update automatically.

Greek, Turkish, Levantine and Israeli cooking are labeled by style. Persian restaurants are included as an adjacent Middle Eastern category and labeled Persian; Iran is not a Mediterranean country. This is a practical dining directory, not a claim that these cuisines are interchangeable. Halal, kosher and dietary suitability should be confirmed with the restaurant.

No customer photographs or full menu images have been copied or republished. External links belong to their respective providers. Independent directory; no ranking or restaurant affiliation.
